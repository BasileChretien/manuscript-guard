# A summary held to the Results

Part of the manuscript-writing skill, read when `check` reports `summary-claim-stronger`.

Many readers read the abstract and nothing else. `check` holds each sentence of the Abstract,
of a conclusion and of a box the journal prints beside the abstract to the Results sentences
that bind the same result, and warns (`summary-claim-stronger`) where it claims more than
every one of them: a hedge or a limit dropped, a word moved up the ladder ("associated" made
"increased"), or a negation gone or new. Put the Results' words back in the summary. Where the
stronger words are right, they belong first in the Results, beside the evidence for them. A
Results sentence that gives the number and no word of the claim ("The adjusted HR was 1.42")
is compared with nothing, so a claim in the sentence before it is not seen: where you can,
write the claim and its number in one sentence.
