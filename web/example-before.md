# Active learning and exam results among second-year students: an example paper

This paper is invented for the audit page, so that a first visit can see what a report looks
like. Its study, its numbers and its references do not exist.

# Introduction

Lectures remain the usual way of teaching in the 2ndyear of medical school [1]. Active
learning asks more of students, and its effect on behaviour in class is debated [2]. The OR
of passing was the main outcome, measured on the visual analogue scale (VAS) of confidence
as well.

# Methods

We invited 240 eligible students. The odds ratio (OR) of passing was estimated by logistic
regression, adjusted for sex. Each session lasted 90min and used a 5mg placebo pill as a
teaching prop. Data were analysed at two study centres, and quizzes were labeled by topic.
Analyses used R 4.3.

# Results

Of the 158 students who took part, 52% were women and 48% were men. Students who joined the
sessions may pass more often (OR 1.6, 95% CI 1.1-2.5, P = 0.01). The colour-coded quizzes
were liked by most.

# Discussion

Active learning was followed by better results, in line with earlier work [2].
