Check a paper's numbers on this computer

This page runs manuscript-guard's audit inside your browser. The paper you choose is read here and is not uploaded: there is no server behind this page to receive it.

manuscript-guard, rebuilt with each new release

Reviewing for a journal? Read its policy first: some forbid putting a manuscript under review into any tool, even one that runs on your own computer.

Loading the checker: about 26 MB the first time, nothing after that.

To see that nothing is uploaded

  
    
    
  • Once the page says Ready, turn off your network. Then choose your paper:
      the audit still runs.
    • 
    
  • The page's own rules let it send requests only to the address it came from, and to
      the five reference registries above when you tick the box. Your browser enforces them.
      
    • 
    
  • Every file the page loads is listed with its SHA-256 in
      files.sha256, and its source is
      on GitHub.

What this page does not check

    
    
      
  • PDFs, LaTeX projects, figures and reproducibility packages. The command line reads
        them, with the same checks: uvx manuscript-guard audit paper.pdf
    • 
      
  • Whether a number is right. A number found in the outputs may be there by chance,
        and the report says how often that would happen.
