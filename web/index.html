Give your paper a friendly check-up

Drop your manuscript here. In a minute or two you get the report a careful co-author would write: numbers that do not add up, abbreviations, spelling and references.

It all runs on this computer. Your paper is never uploaded.

    Questions
    
    
    
      Is my paper uploaded?
      
    No. This page runs manuscript-guard's audit inside your browser, and there
        is no server behind it to receive a file. To see it for yourself:
    
      
      
        
    • Once the page says Ready, turn off your network, then choose your paper:
          the check still runs.
      • 
        
    • The page's own rules let it send requests only to the address it came from, and to
          the five reference registries when you tick that box. Your browser enforces them.
          
      • 
        
    • Every file the page loads is listed with its SHA-256 in
          files.sha256, and its source is
          on GitHub.
      • 
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      What does the check look at?
      
      
        
    • The numbers: against your analysis outputs if you give them, and against each
          other (sums, percentages, the Abstract against the Results, small counts that could
          identify someone).
      • 
        
    • The writing: abbreviations, spelling in one English, how P values, intervals and
          units are written, and phrasing to look at.
      • 
        
    • The reference list: each entry read, and with the box ticked, each DOI and PubMed
          ID looked up for a retraction.
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    
      What does it not check?
      
      
        
    • PDFs, LaTeX projects, figures and reproducibility packages. The command line reads
          them, with the same checks: uvx manuscript-guard audit paper.pdf
      • 
        
    • Whether a number is right. A number found in the outputs may be there by chance,
          and the report says how often that would happen.
      • 
      
    
      
    More: how
        this page works and what its rules cannot stop.
    
    
    
    
    
      Reviewing a paper for a journal?
      
    Read the journal's policy first: some forbid putting a manuscript under review into any
        tool, even one that runs on your own computer.
