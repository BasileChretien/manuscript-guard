Original research

Abstracts should not exceed 300 words.

The main text, from the Introduction to the end of the Discussion, should not exceed 3500
words.

Cite no more than 50 references.

Include no more than four figures and no more than four tables.

Organise the article under the headings Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion.

Every research article must include a data availability statement.

Declare all sources of funding, including where there was none.

All authors must declare their competing interests.

Cite in the Vancouver style, with references numbered in the order they are first cited.

Write in British English throughout.

Observational studies follow the DEMO-OBS checklist, completed and submitted with the
manuscript.

Send a cover letter of no more than one page with every submission.
